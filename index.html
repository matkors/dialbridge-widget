DialBridge · Working prototype

A "Book online" button for home service websites, plus a booking page for their Google profile.

Customers pick a service, see a price range, choose a day and a time window, and leave their number. The owner gets the request by text. One app serves every business; a new client is a few settings, not new code.

How a client installs it

<script async src="https://matviykorsunskiy.me/dialbridge-widget/w.js" data-business="harbor-haul" data-color="#0E6650"></script>

Works on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and Duda. For builders that block floating buttons (like GoDaddy), drop in <div data-dialbridge-inline="harbor-haul"></div> instead. With no website at all, the booking page link goes on Google and Facebook.

Working now
