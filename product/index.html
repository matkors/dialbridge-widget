01 / What your customer sees✳

Three taps to a booked job.

No "call for a quote". Customers see your real services with your photos, get a price range before they commit, and pick a time you actually have open.

01 · Pick the job

Your services, your photos.

Big tap targets that work on a phone. Mixed loads are fine: a junk customer can pick furniture, appliances and bags in one go.

02 · See the price

A real price range, up front.

Ranges come from your own pricing, plus extras like pet hair or heavy items. Big or odd jobs switch to "free estimate" with photos.

03 · Book a time

Only times you can take.

Arrival windows follow your hours, your notice period and how many jobs you can do in a day. Prefer to approve first? Turn bookings into requests.

02 / What they get after booking

Confirmations and reminders, in their own time zone.

Every text says the time the way the customer picked it, so a customer in Louisiana reads 9:00 AM CST, not your 10:00 AM. Each reminder reads differently, so nobody thinks it was sent twice.

07 / Questions

A few things owners ask.

Do I need a new website?

No. The booking button works on the site you already have. If your site is slow or out of date, we can build you a new one as part of the plan.

What if a booking doesn't work for me?

You can set any service to "request" instead of instant booking, so you approve it first. You can also change or cancel from your inbox and the customer is told by text.

Will customers see prices I don't want to commit to?

They see ranges you set, with a note that the final price is confirmed on site. Big or unusual jobs go to "free estimate" with photos instead.

Does it work with my phone number and my calendar?

Yes. Requests come to your phone by text and to your inbox. Calendar and field-software sync (Jobber, Housecall Pro) is on our roadmap.

How fast can it be live?

After a short call, we set up your services, prices and hours. Most of the work is ours, not yours.

Am I locked in?

No. It's month to month and you can cancel any time.
