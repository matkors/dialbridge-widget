Questions

Owners usually ask.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

No. If you like your site, we add the booking button to it. If it's slow or outdated, the new site is included.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. Your number stays on the site and the Call button. Booking just adds a way to reach you when you can't pick up.

What if someone books a job I can't do?

Jobs come in as requests you confirm, so you stay in control. Whole-house cleanouts go to a free walkthrough instead of a price.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. The domain is yours. If you ever leave, it goes with you.

Am I locked in?

No. Month to month, cancel any time.
