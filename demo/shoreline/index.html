Questions

Before you book.

Is the online price the final price?

It is the price we expect to charge. We confirm it when we arrive, before any work starts. If something is different from what you entered, like 8 more windows, we tell you the new number first and you decide.

Do I need to be home?

Not for outside work. Leave the gate unlocked and we text you photos when we finish. For inside windows, someone needs to let us in.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain is fine for windows. For heavy rain or high wind we text you the day before and move you to the next open day. No fee.

Are you insured? Do you take a deposit?

Yes, we carry general liability and workers comp, and we email a certificate if your HOA asks. No deposit. You pay after the job by card or check.
